This privacy policy describes how **Shopping List - Simple Offline List** (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the app. The app is designed to work **offline** for everyday grocery and shopping lists.

---

## Summary

- Your shopping lists, items, categories, favorites, and related app data are stored **on your device**.
- The app does **not** require an account or login.
- We do **not** operate developer-owned servers to store your list data, and we do **not** use developer-owned analytics to collect your shopping content.
- You choose if and when to share a list (for example via WhatsApp) using Android’s share sheet.
- Export and import of catalogue data (CSV) is **manual** and **user-initiated**.
- The app shows **small banner ads** on the **home screen** through **Google AdMob**. Ads are **not** shown during active **shopping mode**.
- **Google** (not us) may process advertising-related data when ads load, according to [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

This summary is for convenience. Please read the full policy below.

---

## 1. Information the app stores on your device

The app stores data locally on your phone or tablet using a local database (SQLite via Room), including for example:

- Grocery items and categories you use or create  
- Favorites and frequent-item counters (based on your use of the app)  
- Shopping session history and related snapshots  
- App settings (such as country/region and language preferences)  

This data stays on your device unless **you** take an action described in later sections (share, export, backup by Android, or delete).

We do **not** upload your grocery list content to developer-operated servers.

---

## 2. Accounts and login

The app does **not** require you to create an account or sign in. There is no user registration flow and no password stored by us for app access.

---

## 3. What we do not collect (developer-operated services)

For the app’s core features, we do **not**:

- Collect or upload your item names, categories, shopping lists, favorites, or shopping history to **our** servers  
- Use **developer-owned** analytics (such as Firebase Analytics) to track your shopping behaviour  
- Automatically share your list data with other people or services  

**Important:** This is not the same as saying “the app collects no data at all.” Third parties such as **Google AdMob** may process limited technical or advertising-related data under their own policies when ads are shown, as described in section 7.

---

## 4. Sharing lists (your choice)

You may **manually** share a shopping list using Android’s standard **share sheet** (for example to another app or contact). Sharing only happens when you tap share and choose a destination.

We do **not** automatically send your lists to anyone. What you share, and with whom, is controlled by you and the app you select (for example a messaging app).

---

## 5. Export and import (CSV)

The app offers **manual** export and import of catalogue data as a **CSV** file:

- Export: you choose where to save the file on your device or cloud storage you control.  
- Import: you choose which file to open; the app reads it only after you select it.  

These actions are **not** automatic backups to our servers. Any copy of a CSV file is under your control (device storage, Google Drive, email attachment, etc.).

---

## 6. Android backup and device transfer

The app is configured so that the **local shopping database** (`grocery_list.db` and related database files) is **excluded** from:

- **Google cloud backup** (where supported by your device and Android version), and  
- **Device-to-device transfer** rules on newer Android versions,  

as defined in the app’s backup configuration files.

**What this means for you:** Your list database is intended to remain on the device and not be included in automatic cloud backup or transfer of that database file by the app’s backup rules. Other Android or OEM backup behaviour may still apply to other apps or system data; we do not control your full device backup settings.

If you want a portable copy of your data, use **CSV export** or another method you trust.

---

## 7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show **banner advertisements** on the **home screen** only.

- **Placement:** Banner ads on the home screen. **No ads** are shown in **shopping mode** (the in-store checklist flow).  
- **Your list data:** We do **not** send your grocery list content to AdMob for targeting.  
- **Google’s role:** When an ad loads, **Google** may collect and process advertising-related data (such as device identifiers, ad interaction, IP address, and technical data) according to [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and, where applicable, [Google’s EU User Consent Policy](https://www.google.com/about/company/user-consent-policy/).  
- **Consent:** In regions where consent is required (for example EEA/UK/Switzerland), the app uses Google’s **User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to collect choices before requesting ads. You can change choices anytime in **Settings → Ad Privacy Options** when available.  
- **No internet:** Core list features work offline. Ads may not load without a network connection; the app should remain usable.  
- **Ad choices:** You can use device and Google ad settings to limit ad personalization. In-app **Settings → Ad Privacy Options** opens Google’s consent tools when available for your region.

Learn more about how Google uses data in advertising:  
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 8. Permissions and network

The app is built for **offline** list management. It may request:

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| **Internet** | Load advertisements (AdMob) |
| **Access network state** | Standard for ad SDKs; check connectivity |

Core grocery list features do **not** require uploading your list to us.

We do **not** use location, contacts, SMS, or camera access for core grocery list features.

## 9. Data retention and deletion

**Delete data instructions (for Google Play):**  
https://github.com/preshan/GroceryList/blob/main/docs/DATA_DELETION.md

### In the app

**Settings → Clear All Local Data** permanently deletes local app data on your device, including items, categories, shopping history, favorites, and app settings stored by the app. This action is intended to be **irreversible** from within the app.

### On your device (Android settings)

You can also remove app data at any time through your device:

**Settings → Apps → Shopping List - Simple Offline List → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data**  
(Wording may vary by device and Android version.)

| Topic | Our position for list data |
|--------|----------------------------|
| Grocery list content | Stored locally; not uploaded to developer servers |
| Account | Not required |
| Developer analytics | Not used for shopping content |
| Sharing | User-initiated via Android share sheet |
| CSV import/export | User-initiated, manual |
