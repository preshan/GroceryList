# Delete your data — Shopping List - Simple Offline List

**App:** Shopping List - Simple Offline List  
**Developer:** Preshan Pradeepa Kariyawasam  
**Contact:** preshanpradeepa@gmail.com  
**Last updated:** 16 May 2026

This page explains how to delete data stored by **Shopping List - Simple Offline List** on your Android device. The app does **not** use accounts or developer-operated servers for your grocery lists.

---

## How to delete your data

### Option 1 — In the app (recommended)

1. Open **Shopping List - Simple Offline List**.
2. Go to **Settings** (from the home screen).
3. Tap **Clear All Local Data**.
4. Read the confirmation message, then confirm.

Deletion is **immediate** on your device. There is no waiting period and no request form required.

### Option 2 — Android system settings

1. Open your device **Settings**.
2. Go to **Apps** → **Shopping List - Simple Offline List** (name may vary slightly by device).
3. Tap **Storage** (or **Storage & cache**).
4. Tap **Clear storage** or **Clear data**.
5. Confirm.

This removes all app data stored on that device.

### Option 3 — Uninstall the app

Uninstalling the app removes its local data from that device, unless your device or another tool keeps a separate backup copy you created yourself (for example a CSV file you exported).

---

## What is deleted

When you use **Clear All Local Data** or clear app storage, the following stored on **that device** is permanently removed:

- Grocery items and categories  
- Store types  
- Favorites and frequent-item statistics  
- Shopping sessions and session snapshots  
- App settings (such as language and region preferences)  
- Other data in the app’s local database  

There is **no** copy of this list data on developer-operated servers to delete, because we do not upload your lists.

---

## What is not deleted by the steps above

| Data | Notes |
|------|--------|
| **Copies you exported** | CSV files or shared list text (for example via WhatsApp) are under **your** control. Delete them in the app or folder where you saved them. |
| **Google AdMob / advertising data** | If you saw ads on the home screen, **Google** may have processed advertising-related data under [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not control Google’s logs. Manage ad settings via **Settings → Ad Privacy Options** in the app (when available) or your device’s Google ad settings. |
| **Other apps** | Data in messaging, cloud storage, or other apps is not affected. |

---

## Retention period

- **Local app data:** Kept on your device until **you** delete it using one of the methods above. We do not set a separate server retention period because we do not store your lists on our servers.  
- **After deletion:** Data removed by Clear All Local Data or clear storage is not recoverable from within the app.  
- **Automatic deletion:** The app does **not** automatically delete your lists after a fixed number of days.

---

## No account required

This app does **not** support user accounts. You do **not** need to email us to delete on-device list data—use the steps above.

If you cannot access the app and need help, contact **preshanpradeepa@gmail.com** with your device model and Android version. We cannot remotely delete data on your phone because we do not receive your list data.
